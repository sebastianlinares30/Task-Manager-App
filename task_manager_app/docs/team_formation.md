### Role Responsibilities

#### Scrum Master
- Facilitate sprint planning and team meetings.
- Help remove blockers and obstacles.
- Ensure Scrum practices are followed.
- Keep the team focused on sprint goals.

#### Developer
- Implement assigned features and user stories.
- Collaborate on coding tasks.
- Participate in code reviews.
- Update progress regularly.

#### Tester
- Verify completed features.
- Identify and report bugs.
- Perform acceptance testing.
- Confirm requirements are met before completion.

#### Meeting Recorder
- Take notes during meetings.
- Document decisions and action items.
- Share meeting summaries with the team.
- Maintain project records and documentation.
