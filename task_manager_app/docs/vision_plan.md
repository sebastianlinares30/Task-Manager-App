


## Our Core Vision

We are here to help CS majors stop missing commitments and breaking promises. Between mountains of coding assignments and maintaining a social life, things slip through the cracks. This app helps students stay on top of it all.

-   **Target User:** College students (specifically CS majors).
    
-   **Pain Points We Are Fixing:** missed commitments, and accidental broken promises.
    
-   **Our Core Values:** Uplifting, supportive, and encouraging.

## Risks Mitigation

### 1. Missing Our Deadlines

-   **The Risk:** Someone gets stuck on a hard coding problem or falls behind schedule.
    
-   **The Fix:** We will set up extra emergency meetings outside of our regular class times to help anyone who is falling behind get caught up quickly.
    

### 2. Scope Creep (Adding Too Many Features)

-   **The Risk:** We get excited and try to add way too many extra features, which ruins our timeline.
    
-   **The Fix:** We will strictly stick to our weekly sprint plans. Any "cool new ideas" will be tossed into a backlog folder to look at later.
    

### 3. Live Presentation Glitches

-   **The Risk:** The app crashes or breaks right while we are giving our final presentation to the class.
    
-   **The Fix:** We built a 1.5-week buffer into our timeline just to hunt down bugs. This gives us plenty of time to foolproof the entire app before the big day.
